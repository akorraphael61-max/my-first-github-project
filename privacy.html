Privacy Policy

How contact messages and visitor data are handled on this site.

Summary

This site collects the name, email address, and message when you submit the contact form. Submissions are forwarded to the site owner or handled by a third-party form service (Formspree) if configured. This page explains what is collected, why, and how to request deletion.

What we collect

  • Name — provided in the contact form.
  • Email address — provided in the contact form to allow the site owner to reply.
  • Message — the content you send via the contact form.
  • Technical metadata — when you visit the site the host (GitHub Pages) may log basic request metadata such as IP address, user-agent, and timestamp. This site does not track visitors with analytics by default.

How messages are processed

By default this site uses a Formspree placeholder. If you (the site owner) replace the placeholder with a real Formspree form endpoint, form submissions will be sent to Formspree and forwarded to the configured email address. Formspree is a third-party service with its own privacy and processing policies (Formspree Privacy).

If the placeholder is still present, the site shows a local demo confirmation and the message is not sent to an external service.

Why we collect this data

To enable visitors to contact the site owner and to reply to inquiries. The email address is used only for contacting the sender and is not shared publicly.

Retention and deletion

If messages are forwarded to Formspree or another email provider, those services may retain copies according to their policies. To request deletion of a message stored by the site owner, please email: youremail@example.com. If Formspree is used, please refer to Formspree’s documentation for their data deletion procedures.

Legal basis (EU/UK GDPR)

If you are in the EU/UK, the legal basis for processing your contact message is your consent when you submit the form and the legitimate interest of the site owner in responding to inquiries.

Security

This is a static site served over HTTPS by GitHub Pages. Form submissions to Formspree are sent over HTTPS. The site owner follows basic security practices, but please avoid sending sensitive personal information through the contact form.

Third-party services

This site may use Formspree (third-party) to process form submissions. Formspree’s privacy policy is: https://formspree.io/privacy. If you configure other services (analytics, hosting, email providers), their policies will also apply.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal data. To exercise these rights, contact the site owner at youremail@example.com. If a third-party service processed your message, please also consult their support and privacy documentation.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy or want to request data deletion, please contact: youremail@example.com.
